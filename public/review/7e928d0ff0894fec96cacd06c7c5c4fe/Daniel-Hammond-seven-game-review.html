Corsi share is calculated as Corsi+ / (Corsi+ + Corsi-). Hudl defines these Corsi counts as even-strength team shot attempts while the player is on the ice. They describe the unit's results, not individual blame. Do not divide them by total ice time to create an even-strength rate.

1. Keep the Chicago game as a positive reference

Daniel had 9 shots, 8 on goal, his only recorded goal, and a +9 Corsi differential in the 4-1 Chicago win. That game accounts for 8 of his 18 shots on goal. Across the other six games, he put 10 of 20 attempts on goal (50%). Ask what was repeatable about his shot preparation and support play rather than expecting the Chicago scoring output every game.

2. Investigate how the team gets out of pressure

Across all seven games, the Corsi counts were 70 for and 110 against (38.9% share). Five games were negative. In the four October games the counts were 29 for and 66 against (30.5%), compared with 41 for and 44 against (48.2%) in September. Opposition, teammates, deployment and game state differ; this is a descriptive pattern, not proof of regression or a Daniel-specific fault.

On-ice team xG was 4.78 for and 8.58 against across all seven reports. These are on-ice unit measures. They do not identify whether the underlying issue was retrievals, exits, gap control, coverage, teammate support, or something else. Start by reviewing the Vernon Hills 2-3 game, where the counts were 7/23 and xG was 0.34/1.80.

3. Make shot execution more consistent

He put 6 of 12 attempts on goal in October. His two Naperville reports show 2/2 and 4/1: accuracy varied even against the same opponent. For a defenseman, the useful question is whether he sees the lane and makes a productive shot-or-pass choice. Low personal xG is not automatically a flaw for a player shooting from the point, and this model does not measure every rebound, screen or passing option.

4. Use his defensive involvement without rewarding more defending

The reports record 10 blocked shots, including 3 in the Vernon Hills 2-3 game. Keep acknowledging useful defensive plays. More blocks can also reflect more time under pressure, so do not set a target to increase blocks. Likewise, do not make his InStat Index or plus/minus the practice objective.
