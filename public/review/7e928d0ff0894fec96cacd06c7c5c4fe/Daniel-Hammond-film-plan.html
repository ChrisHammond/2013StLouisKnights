Practice tonight · use the drills your coach runs

Tell your coach: “I want to work on competing for pucks, getting more shots off and checking around me. Can you give me one reminder tonight?”

  1. Passing and shooting reps: ready before it arrives. Get your skates up ice, show your stick, look at the lane and receive ready to release. Shoot promptly when the lane is available. If it is covered, make a small adjustment or a pass and get available again. Start with this cue: Ready feet.
  2. Retrievals and defensive reps: find a threat and a teammate. Check over your shoulder before arriving at the puck. Away from the puck, briefly check the middle/back-side threat and your support, then recheck as the puck moves. Use the team's coverage rules. Cue: Threat and teammate.
  3. Existing battle or small-area drills: engage. Move your feet into the contest, establish position and work to win or recover possession. Ask the coach to correct your physical technique. Cue: Feet into the battle. Practice this in coached drills; do not chase hits or leave your assignment to force contact.
